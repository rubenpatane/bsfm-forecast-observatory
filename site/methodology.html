Preregistered research protocol

Designed to make retrospective success difficult.

The central unit is not a story about an accident. It is an immutable forecast made from an admissible point-in-time snapshot, followed by an outcome and a predefined score.

Research object

Immutable chain of evidence

1 · source snapshot2 · cutoff3 · admissibility4 · frozen prediction5 · future outcome6 · scoring7 · calibration8 · baseline comparison
Target

Next fatal Boeing commercial-jet accident

Historical cases use strict next-event-after-cutoff semantics. If another qualifying target lies between a cutoff and the nominal event, that nominal case is not a valid next-event case for that cutoff.

Dimensions

Predefined, not selected after outcome

timefamilyexact modelphasefailure/event classcoarse geography

Operator, registration and MSN are not scored unless separately supported and prospectively preregistered.

Candidate estimator

Shrinkage hazard

A deliberately low-complexity cohort estimator regularizes sparse event counts toward a pooled exposure-normalized rate. It is implemented but cannot be fitted by the lifecycle until the evidence gate opens.

Null model

Exposure only

Cohort probability is normalized within the relevant period from defensible departures exposure. Fleet share and global traffic are not silently substituted.

Evaluation

Paired OOS

Candidate and baseline must share identical case IDs and outcomes. Brier and multiclass Brier are proper scores; reliability is reported only from real OOS predictions.

F-002 is not validation evidence by itself.

It is a prospective experimental record. Apparent later similarities do not establish predictive skill. The study earns that claim only through repeated, preregistered, out-of-sample scoring against a reference model.

Method changes are versioned. Frozen forecasts and failed evaluations are not rewritten.
