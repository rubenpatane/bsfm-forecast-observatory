Open prospective research · auditable by design

Can public aviation data forecast risk beyond exposure alone?

BSFM is a preregistered research observatory. It freezes forecasts before outcomes, enforces point-in-time evidence, scores failures as well as successes, and refuses to convert missing evidence into certainty.

Experimental research — not an operational safety tool.
BSFM does not assess the safety of a specific flight, aircraft, airline, route or person. No predictive-validity claim is allowed until leakage-free historical and prospective evidence passes the published gates.
