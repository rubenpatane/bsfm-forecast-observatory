BSFM Research Observatory

A prospective, auditable experiment asking whether point-in-time public aviation data contain predictive information beyond simple exposure baselines.

Experimental research only.
This observatory is not a certification or operational decision tool. It does not assess the safety of an individual flight, aircraft, airline, route or person. Apparent matches can occur by chance; predictive skill must be demonstrated out of sample.
