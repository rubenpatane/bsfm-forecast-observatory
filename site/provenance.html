Exposure integrity

No denominator by convenience

Global departures, fleet counts and accident counts cannot be proportionally allocated to Boeing families merely to make the baseline computable. Missing annual cohort exposure remains a visible scientific gap.

Census integrity

Zero is evidence too

A year with no qualifying event must still be explicitly reconciled. Absence of a row is not accepted as proof of zero qualifying accidents.
