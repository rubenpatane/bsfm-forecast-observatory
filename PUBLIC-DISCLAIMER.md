# Public Research Disclaimer

**BSFM Forecast Observatory is a research exercise, not a real-world aviation-safety prediction service.**

BSFM explores probabilistic methods on public aviation data. It does not predict whether a specific aircraft, flight, airline, route, date, or location will suffer an accident. Outputs are experimental statistical hypotheses and must not be used for travel, operational, maintenance, investment, regulatory, or safety decisions.

The model is experimental and unvalidated. Apparent matches can occur by chance. Predictive value, if any, must be established through preregistered out-of-sample evaluation, calibration, and comparison with baseline models.

Forecast-like hypotheses are frozen before evaluation and are not rewritten to fit later events. Outcomes are recorded separately. Historical hypotheses that predate this repository are explicitly identified as reconstructed; repository timestamps must not be represented as proof of earlier public publication.

Passing software tests does not establish predictive skill. Missing evidence must remain unknown rather than being inferred or fabricated.

For real operational aviation-safety decisions, rely on aircraft operators and competent aviation authorities.
